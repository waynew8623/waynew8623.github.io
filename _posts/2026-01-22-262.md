<h2 style="align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; box-shadow: none; display: block; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; line-height: 1.5em; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; padding: 0px; margin: 30px 0px 15px 0px; border: 1px none #000000;"><span style="font-size: 18px; color: #0e8aeb; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; box-shadow: none; display: block; font-weight: bold; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; border-width: 1px 1px 1px 5px; border-color: #000000 #000000 #000000 #0e88eb; border-style: none none none solid; padding: 0px 0px 0px 10px; margin: 0px;">读取 RSS 数据源</span></h2>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">我们使用 <a href="https://waynetalk.com/524/">RSS Read 节点</a>来读取热点新闻数据源。</p>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">搜索 RSS，找到 RSS Read 节点。</p>

<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">在 RSS Read 节点中，输入 RSS 源 URL，比如：https://36kr.com/feed，也可以填入其他你关注的 RSS 源地址。</p>

<h2 style="align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; box-shadow: none; display: block; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; line-height: 1.5em; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; padding: 0px; margin: 30px 0px 15px 0px; border: 1px none #000000;"><span style="font-size: 18px; color: #0e8aeb; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; box-shadow: none; display: block; font-weight: bold; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; border-width: 1px 1px 1px 5px; border-color: #000000 #000000 #000000 #0e88eb; border-style: none none none solid; padding: 0px 0px 0px 10px; margin: 0px;">过滤节点：筛选需要的内容</span></h2>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">热点信息太多了，有些内容可能并不是我们关注的领域，我们需要过滤出真正需要的内容。</p>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">输入 filter，找到 Filter 节点。</p>
<figure style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: center; align-items: center; padding: 0px; margin: 10px 0px 10px 0px;"><img decoding="async" class="rich_pages wxw-img" style="display: block; max-width: 100%; border-top-left-radius: 8px; border-top-right-radius: 8px; border-bottom-right-radius: 8px; border-bottom-left-radius: 8px; object-fit: fill; box-shadow: #85a1c9 0px 0px 0px 0px; border-width: 3px; border-color: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4); border-style: none; margin: 0px auto 0px auto;" src="https://waynetalk.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/640-0c4cba1f.png" data-ratio="0.6746666666666666" data-type="png" data-w="750" data-imgfileid="100003854" data-aistatus="1" /></figure>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">在过滤节点中，设置过滤条件，比如只保留包含特定关键词的文章，或者排除某些不相关的内容。</p>
<figure style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: center; align-items: center; padding: 0px; margin: 10px 0px 10px 0px;"><img decoding="async" class="rich_pages wxw-img" style="display: block; max-width: 100%; border-top-left-radius: 8px; border-top-right-radius: 8px; border-bottom-right-radius: 8px; border-bottom-left-radius: 8px; object-fit: fill; box-shadow: #85a1c9 0px 0px 0px 0px; border-width: 3px; border-color: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4); border-style: none; margin: 0px auto 0px auto;" src="https://waynetalk.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/640-30159931.png" data-ratio="0.6814814814814815" data-type="png" data-w="1080" data-imgfileid="100003855" data-aistatus="1" /></figure>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">这样，我们就只获取真正需要关注的热点内容了。</p>
<h2 style="align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; box-shadow: none; display: block; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; line-height: 1.5em; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; padding: 0px; margin: 30px 0px 15px 0px; border: 1px none #000000;"><span style="font-size: 18px; color: #0e8aeb; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; border-top-left-radius: 0px; border-top-right-radius: 0px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0px; border-bottom-right-radius: 0px; align-items: unset; background-attachment: scroll; background-clip: border-box; background-color: unset; background-image: none; background-origin: padding-box; background-position-x: 0%; background-position-y: 0%; background-repeat: no-repeat; background-size: auto; box-shadow: none; display: block; font-weight: bold; flex-direction: unset; float: unset; height: auto; justify-content: unset; overflow-x: unset; overflow-y: unset; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; text-shadow: none; transform: none; width: auto; -webkit-box-reflect: unset; border-width: 1px 1px 1px 5px; border-color: #000000 #000000 #000000 #0e88eb; border-style: none none none solid; padding: 0px 0px 0px 10px; margin: 0px;">限制数量：控制输出规模</span></h2>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">如果 RSS 源中的文章很多，我们可以限制输出的数量，避免一次性处理太多内容。</p>
<p style="color: #000000; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.8em; letter-spacing: 0em; text-align: left; text-indent: 0em; padding: 8px 0px 8px 0px; margin: 0px;">输入 limit，找到 Limit 节点。</p>
